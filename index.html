Privacy Policy — Willpower

Last updated: November 2, 2025

Introduction

Willpower ("we", "our", "the app") is a habit-breaking app developed by Ramans Labs. This Privacy Policy explains what data the app collects, how it is used, and your rights.

Developer: Ramans Labs
Contact: ramanslabs@gmail.com

What data we collect

Data stored on your device only

The following data never leaves your device and is not sent to any server: habits, streaks, relapse triggers, notes, and app preferences (language, theme, currency). This data is stored locally in your device's storage and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Data sent to third parties

DeepSeek (AI chat): When you use the SOS chat, your messages are sent to the DeepSeek API to generate responses. Messages are not stored on our servers. DeepSeek's privacy policy applies: https://www.deepseek.com/privacy

Google AdMob (advertising): The app displays ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect your advertising ID to serve personalized ads. You can opt out of personalized ads in your device settings. AdMob's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

Data we do NOT collect

We do not require an account, do not collect your name or phone number, do not track your location, do not access your contacts or files, and do not use analytics or crash reporting services.

Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Your rights

Since we do not store any personal data on our servers, all your data is under your direct control. You can delete all data at any time via Settings → Delete All Data, or uninstall the app to remove everything permanently.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top will reflect changes.

Contact

If you have questions, contact us at: ramanslabs@gmail.com
